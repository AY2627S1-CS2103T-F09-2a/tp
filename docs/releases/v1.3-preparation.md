The local merge of current master and #79 fails compilation at `SetOutstandingCommand.java`:
`Optional<Phone> cannot be converted to Phone`. The locally tested fee integration patch unwraps
`getPhone()`, `getEmail()` and `getAddress()` with `.orElse(null)` when constructing the updated student.
That patch depends on #79's API and must not be applied to current master before #79 lands.

With that local patch, the #79 snapshot passes its full tests and Checkstyle against current master.
A local combination of #79, #84 and #85 also passes full tests and Checkstyle.
An additional parsed-command workflow verifies required guardian fields, omitted optional fields, normalized
name/guardian-phone duplicates, invalid parameters, add/find/view, invalid-search filter preservation,
set/clear fees, deletion from a fees list and reload of the remaining student.
This uses #79's formatter; #83's conflicting formatter has not been integrated or declared resolved.
GUI field formatting, visible selection and final screenshots still require final-product verification.
