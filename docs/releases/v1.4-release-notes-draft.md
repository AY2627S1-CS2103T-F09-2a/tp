## Changes since v1.3

### 1. Assertions are enabled when the app runs

`build.gradle` sets `applicationDefaultJvmArgs = ['-ea']`, so `./gradlew run` and the
packaged JAR launched with `java -ea -jar` both execute Java assertions. `docs/SettingUp.md`
documents how to add `-ea` to a direct IntelliJ run configuration. Assertion failures now
stop the app instead of passing silently (verified with a deliberate `assert false`, which
produced `java.lang.AssertionError` and exit status 1 under both launch paths).

### 2. Total outstanding fees across all students: `feesummary`

```text
feesummary
```

Reports `N student(s) owe a total of S$AMOUNT.` with exactly two decimal places, counting
**every stored student**, not just the displayed list. It is a read-only command: the filter,
the selection and the saved data are untouched, and it does not write to storage. Extra
arguments are rejected. Two students owing S$180.00 and S$75.50 produce
`2 student(s) owe a total of S$255.50.`; no fees produce `0 student(s) owe a total of S$0.00.`

Totals use `BigDecimal`, so they cannot drift by a cent and may legitimately exceed the
S$99999.99 per-student cap.

### 3. Record a full payment: `paid INDEX`

```text
paid INDEX
```

Clears the outstanding fee of the student at `INDEX` in the currently displayed list and
reports `Marked outstanding fee as paid for NAME: S$AMOUNT`. A typical flow is `fees` to see
who owes money, then `paid 1` for the student who paid.

- Every other field of the student (optional phone, email, address, guardian name and phone,
  tags) is preserved, and the active filter stays in place.
- A paid student disappears from a `fees` result and the remaining students are re-indexed;
  in a `find` result the student stays visible if they still match.
- A student without a fee is rejected with
  `This student has no outstanding fee to mark as paid: NAME`.
- `paid 0`, `paid 01`, `paid +1`, `paid 1 2` and out-of-range indexes are rejected without
  changing data or the displayed list.
- `setoutstanding INDEX 0` remains available for clearing a fee explicitly.

### 4. Documentation

- The User Guide documents `feesummary` and `paid` and lists both in the command summary.
- The Developer Guide explains how both commands work, why the summary reads the unfiltered
  book, why the query opts out of saving, and which alternatives were rejected. It adds
  UC07/UC08 and manual-testing steps for both commands.
- `ModelClassDiagram` now shows `GuardianName`, `GuardianPhone` and `OutstandingFee`, and
  marks `Phone`, `Email`, `Address` and `OutstandingFee` as `0..1`.

## Data compatibility

The storage schema is unchanged since v1.3: no migration is needed, and a v1.3
`data/addressbook.json` loads as-is. A v1.4 file also loads in v1.3 because no new fields
are persisted.

## Known limitations

- Fees are a single outstanding amount per student; there is no payment history or partial
  payment. `paid` clears the whole amount.
- `paid` acts on the displayed list, so indexes shift after a payment; re-run `fees` before
  paying a second student.
- `feesummary` cannot be limited to a filtered subset.
- Saving failures are reported, but the in-memory change may remain until the app closes.
- GUI screenshots for the release page must be captured by a human on a real display; the
  agent session cannot capture the window.
